---
title: Nothing More Than a Thank-You
date: 2026-10-16
categories: [Reflection]
tags: [kindness, expectations]
description: A small note on kindness, silence, and the things I still expect in return.
---

I like to think I'm a nice guy. I compliment people when I get the chance.
I do something nice for somebody without expecting anything more than a
thank-you.

That last bit is where I get stuck.

The other day, I complimented a guy on his jacket in the elevator.
He didn't look in my direction. He just walked out. I didn't know how to
respond, or even how to take it. Maybe he didn't hear me. I don't know.
But I noticed that the silence bothered me.

Afterwards, I caught myself thinking: I keep saying I don't expect anything.
But a thank-you is something.

I haven't reached that ultimate Zen state where I can do something nice
and feel completely fine when nothing comes back. I can say I don't need
anything in return, but apparently I still want someone to acknowledge it.

Recently, I wrote a small note in my actual notebook and emailed it as an
attachment to someone I respect in the field. I haven't heard back.
And yes, I felt let down. Not a huge downer, but enough to notice.

I wasn't hoping for anything personal. Again, I thought, just a thank-you.
There was that word again: *just*.

Not getting a reply is a possibility I have to accept, even when I hope
for one. Knowing that hasn't stopped the disappointment yet.

I don't know if I'll ever reach that Zen state. For now, I'm noticing
what I expect, especially when I tell myself I expect nothing.

Here's to learning more about oneself.
